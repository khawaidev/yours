Get the Yours app

Install Yours to your home screen — it works just like a native app, no app store needed.

Install on Android

One tap — Chrome will handle the rest.

No install button? Open your browser menu () and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Install on iPhone & iPad

Three quick steps in Safari:

  1. 1Tap the  Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add — Yours appears on your home screen.
