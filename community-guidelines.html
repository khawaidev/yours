Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 2025 · Version 1.0

These guidelines exist so Yours stays a safe, respectful place for an adult audience. They apply to your conversations with AI characters and to your conduct in the app. We may moderate or suspend accounts that violate them, in line with our Terms of Service.

1. You must be 18+

The Service is for adults only. Do not claim to be, or attempt to interact as, anyone under 18. Do not attempt to make characters appear underage — accounts doing so will be removed.

2. Remember the characters are AI

Characters are fictional. Don't present them as real people, and don't use them to impersonate or defame real individuals. Never attempt to extract a real person's identity or details through a character.

3. No harassment, hate or abuse

Discrimination, slurs, threats, and encouraging violence or self-harm are prohibited anywhere in the app. We support a safe space to explore adult themes, but not content designed to distress or harm.

4. No non-consensual or extreme content

Content depicting or promoting non-consent, minors, or illegal acts is strictly forbidden. This includes imagery, text, and voice conversations.

5. Respect payment and platform integrity

Don't attempt to abuse payments, refunds, credits, or promotional features, and don't try to bypass the app's security, rate limits or content controls.

6. Be honest about yourself

Don't create accounts with someone else's identity, provide false age or other details used for safety, or impersonate Yours staff.

7. Reporting

If you believe a character, conversation, or account violates these guidelines, contact us at khawain.in@gmail.com. We review reports promptly and take action where warranted.

8. Consequences

Violations may result in warnings, removal of content, suspension, or permanent termination of your account. Repeated or severe violations will lead to termination. We may also report serious illegal content to the proper authorities.
