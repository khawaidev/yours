Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2025 · Version 1.0

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser, and similar technologies (such as local storage), that remember information about your visit. This policy explains how these are used in the Yours app.

2. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for the app to function, such as keeping you signed in, remembering your session and preferences.
  • Authentication storage: we store your sign-in token in local storage so your session persists between visits. Removing this signs you out.
  • Preferences: choices like your filters, language and onboarding state are stored locally on your device.
  • Analytics and performance: where used (for example, to understand how features are used), data is anonymised or minimised.

3. Third-party cookies

We use services that may set their own cookies or storage, including authentication and payment providers and CDNs that host app images. These providers treat this data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent core features — such as sign-in and saved conversations — from working. You can also clear stored data at any time from your browser's settings.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in the app.
